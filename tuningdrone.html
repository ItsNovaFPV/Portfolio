Tuning Drone

A steady tone to tune and play against. Tap a note to start it. It keeps sounding until you stop it, with no account and no time limit.

Octave

A pure sine tone, tuned to A = 440. Tap a key to start it and tap it again to stop. Space starts and stops, ← → change the note, ↑ ↓ move the keyboard an octave. On an iPhone, switch the ringer on if you hear nothing.
