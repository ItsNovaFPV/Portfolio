Tuning Drone

A steady tone to tune and play against. Pick a note and an octave and press play. It keeps sounding until you stop it, with no account and no time limit.

Pure is a plain sine wave. Warm adds a few soft overtones so low notes still come through laptop and Chromebook speakers. Space starts and stops, ← → change the note, ↑ ↓ change the octave. On an iPhone, switch the ringer on if you hear nothing.
